CMPC and CRIGF in 3D

Rotate, zoom and edit the devices from the schematics: the chaotic multipass cell in a free-standing SiNx membrane, with a cutaway through its trench DBRs, an integrated Herriott cell with curved trench-DBR mirrors, and the CRIGF (DBR | phase | grating coupler | phase | DBR) on thin-film LiNbO₃. Drag to rotate, scroll or pinch to zoom, right-drag or two-finger drag to pan.

Device

New design (QUIET Task 11): a free-space beam on a large-area input grating coupler, its angle (±60 mrad) and position (±100 µm) swept by a scanning mirror off the chip; 8 index inclusions of r 25 µm, output on the facet the launch orbit aims at.

Membrane cell

Fewer periods over the port width let part of the light through into a tapered strip-loaded waveguide.

Integrated Herriott cell

Spots xn = A cos(nθ + φ). At 0° (port at the edge) the exit ray retraces the input; 90° puts the port at the mirror centre and opens a V between input and output.

Chip drawing (schematic)
