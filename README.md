# misc-applets

Self-contained interactive applets, one `.html` file each, no build step, on top of a shared set of tested calculation engines in `math-engines/`.

| Applet | Description |
|---|---|
| [parametric-amplifier.html](parametric-amplifier.html) | Fiber parametric amplifier: coupled-wave RK4 solver for χ⁽²⁾ (532 nm pump) and χ⁽³⁾ (degenerate pump) OPAs with a signal near 1550 nm. LP01 fiber dispersion, gain bandwidth and idler spectrum, continuous/lumped and spectrally engineered idler loss, Brillouin and Raman scattering, and bandwidth optimisation over loss, fiber geometry, length and pump power. |
| [er-waveguide-amplifier.html](er-waveguide-amplifier.html) | Er:Al₂O₃ strip-loaded waveguide amplifier on Al₂O₃, Si₃N₄, TFLN or TFLT. Er concentration, strip and film dimensions, length, pump 980/1480 nm; pump and signal overlaps with the doped strip (2D finite-difference modes), steady-state rate equations with upconversion, pump/signal propagation, net gain spectrum, gain vs length, pump power and Er concentration, optional concentration-dependent upconversion and ion quenching, and an optimizer for strip/film dimensions, concentration and length. Moved here from `physics-applets`. |

| [cmpc-ray-tracer.html](cmpc-ray-tracer.html) | Multi-beam ray tracer for a segmented circular multipass cell: number of beams, centre angle (slider or pointer steering in the cell view) and fan width, star-orbit aiming, global facet curvature, random tilt and curvature perturbations, single-facet tilt/curvature, optional input/output ports; mirror reflectance with intensity Rʲ and effective path Σ Rʲ ℓⱼ; phase vs launch angle per pass and fringe visibility under sine/triangle angle dither, explained pass by pass by the dither depth x = a_p A (J₀/sinc model), phase chirp and facet-path switching, with adaptive sampling and the random-phase noise floor (`ray_phase` engine, run in a background worker); etched-trench DBR mirrors with R(χ) applied at every hit, angle-of-incidence distribution and the reflectance the light actually sees compared with the unperturbed cell (`cell_mirror` engine); figure view with a tight scale bar; phase-space (Poincaré) plot and twin-ray Lyapunov estimate. Geometry is a JS port of `billiard_cell.SegmentedCell`, checked against the Python test vectors. |
| [fringe-washout.html](fringe-washout.html) | Fringe Washout Planner: how angle dithering, laser-frequency dithering (sine or triangle), temperature and laser drifts, laser linewidth and averaging over µs to 100 s reduce the interference fringe from a stray path; residual fringe vs averaging time against an absorbance target, where the fringe power goes, the static floor that averaging cannot remove (including intermodulation products when the dither rates share harmonics, with a detune button), and the fringe signal over time. Exact harmonic expansion (`fringe_averaging` engine), run in a background worker. |
| [planar-mpc-ray-tracer.html](planar-mpc-ray-tracer.html) | Planar MPC Ray Tracer: the CMPC ray tracer's analyses for other planar (on-chip) cells. Smooth stadium, stadium with faceted caps and segmented straights, circle, segmented polygon, and the integrated Herriott cell (two concave in-plane mirrors, re-entrant design, one window for input and output, pattern phase φ). Random and single-element tilt, curvature and normal-offset perturbations (caps or straights only for the stadium); beams, fan and pointer steering; ports; intensity and effective path; Monte-Carlo port statistics against the ergodic mean field; trench-DBR reflectance over the cell's angles vs the unperturbed cell; Poincaré section and twin-ray Lyapunov estimate; phase vs launch angle and dither visibility. For the Herriott cell: design numbers, mode radius, spot spacing and window clearance, traced spots against A cos(nθ + φ), and a re-entrance tolerance sweep over M2 radius, spacing, M2 tilt or launch angle (`planar_cell`, `ray_phase`, `cell_mirror` engines, background workers). |
| [onchip-herriott.html](onchip-herriott.html) | On-chip Herriott Cell: the integrated (in-plane) Herriott cell with its Gaussian mode on every pass. Effective path, mode volume (effective transverse size × effective path) and Γ-weighted gas overlap, power-weighted mean/RMS/max angle of incidence, throughput with window coupling and Gaussian clipping; mirrors as a constant R or an etched-trench DBR (periods, orders, design angle, slab polarisation) at each hit's angle or averaged over the beam's angular spectrum; sweeps against mirror reflectance and DBR periods; closed-cell option (L_eff → ℓ̄/(1 − R)); M2 radius, tilt and spacing errors; waveguide loss in dB/cm with an L_eff-against-cell-size comparison; a solved layer stack (Al₂O₃, Si₃N₄, TFLN, TFLT or Si film on SiO₂ or as a membrane, with an optional Er:Al₂O₃ layer) with the mode's overlap in every layer; input-beam collimation (waist and focus) with Gaussian beam propagation, round-trip stability, coupling and breathing; and an Er:Al₂O₃ amplifier (980/1480 nm pump) in which crossing passes share the inversion: pump, signal and forward/backward ASE of all passes add up in the Er layer and are solved self-consistently, plus spontaneous emission into all other in-plane directions (all lateral modes), ray-traced through the closed cell with the mirrors' R(λ, χ), absorbed or amplified wherever it crosses inverted Er, with parasitic lasing of mirror-trapped orbits clamping the gain and a back-trace of the output mode for the off-axis ASE that reaches the signal; gain with and without it, in-plane ASE budget and flux map, noise figure, ASE spectra, a 2D inversion map, gain along the folded path and against pump power, and independent-pass and flat-top models for comparison (`onchip_herriott` engine). |
| [gas-cell-ray-tracer.html](gas-cell-ray-tracer.html) | Gas Cell Ray Tracer: an elongated on-chip Herriott cell (e.g. 15 × 1.5 mm) with smooth, deformable mirrors (conic, cubic and quartic deformation, optional reflecting side walls), curved segmented mirrors (segment curvature, tilt jitter), rippled end mirrors (stochasticity K = 8π²aL/Λ²) and refractive-index inclusions inside the cell (refraction, TIR, optical path) as the baseline against a circular multipass cell of the same footprint, both fed by the same Gaussian beam. Path gained against chaos lost: detected power, mean path and absorbance per ppm of a CH₄/NH₃/CO₂/H₂O line; twin-ray Lyapunov exponent, launch-dither fringe visibility pass by pass and Poincaré sections; a tangent-map (FTLE, MEGNO) chaos map of the input mirror that shows whether the beam launches into the chaotic sea or onto a regular island; a Gaussian-beam two-mirror (ABCD) model along the traced path with the local curvature at every hit (beam radius, power kept in one beam, round-trip stability); M² of the detected light; shot-noise and fringe-limited detection limits; speckle (fringe) suppression in dB with log-scale visibility plots; opens on a closed 10 × 1.5 mm cell with 0.3 µm ripple (K ≈ 0.95); launch re-aiming and a sweep over any perturbation (`gas_cell`, `planar_cell`, `ray_phase` engines). |
| [multipass-chaos-lab.html](multipass-chaos-lab.html) | Multipass Chaos Lab: (A) a circular multipass cell and (B) an integrated Herriott cell side by side, each with random refractive-index inclusions (number, radius, |Δn|, sign, seed; in the circular cell optionally only outside the launch caustic a sin θ), facet and mirror perturbations under an advanced section. Ray-tracing figures in matching square frames, dense Poincaré sections of the current and the regular cells, twin-ray and renormalised-twin (FTLE, MEGNO) chaos indicators; residual fringe against averaging time for no dither, angle dither, wavelength dither and both, from the 2D harmonic spectrum of the light leaving the output port (each dither angle followed to its own exit, amplitude-weighted; coherence-weighted samples: exact Filon where the exit phase is smooth, random-phase estimate where it is scrambled); static visibility of regular and current cells (bars, both cells on one axis), fringe suppression without spectral blur (residual fringe against the wavelength-dither amplitude with the gas line's Voigt half-width at a chosen pressure, and the wavelength dither each cell needs for the target in line half-widths against pressure: angle dithering of chaotic cells keeps the laser frequency fixed), an inclusion scan, a configuration study of visibility against the stretching λ·n (Lyapunov exponent × mean exit pass) with a common exponential fit; speckle diversity N_eff over angle × wavelength dither amplitude; sweeps over inclusion and dither parameters (wavelength amplitude to 1 nm, stray-path share); every figure downloads as its own vector PDF with an embedded font (jsPDF from cdnjs on first use). Built on the `gas_cell` and `planar_cell` JS ports; the dither and speckle model (DitherLab) is new and not yet backed by a Python engine. |
| [multipass-chaos-figure.html](multipass-chaos-figure.html) | Multipass Chaos Figure: a paper-figure bench in the style of the Fig. 6 Playground, running the Multipass Chaos Lab engine (DitherLab) on the lab's defaults: ray traces of the regular and chaotic circular cell (CMPC) and Herriott side views at true scale, a 2 × 2 grid of Poincaré sections (Herriott and CMPC, regular and chaotic), and the static residual fringe 2√ε V by dither (none, angle, wavelength, both) for the four cells on a log axis. Figure size in cm, Arial text at a chosen size, colours per cell, vector or 600 dpi raster maps; exports to vector PDF (embedded Liberation Sans, jsPDF from cdnjs) and SVG, plus the bar values as CSV and the full settings as JSON (rail, the engine options of all four cells, figure and display settings, bar values; [example from the default run](multipass-chaos-figure_settings.json)). |
| [herriott-ray-tracer.html](herriott-ray-tracer.html) | Herriott Cell Ray Tracer: exact 3D tracing of spherical (Herriott) and astigmatic (Lissajous) two-mirror cells with mirror-2 tilt, decentre, radius, astigmatism, spacing, conic and coma-like surface errors and injection-angle offsets; spot patterns on both mirrors with mode-radius footprints and the paraxial design, side views, re-entrance check (exit offset in hole radii, re-entry angle, spot spacing and hole clearance in mode radii), re-entrance tolerance sweeps, intensity and effective path, twin-ray separation, and phase vs injection angle with dither visibility (`herriott_cell`, `ray_phase` engines). |
| Standalone builds | [cmpc-ray-tracer-standalone.html](cmpc-ray-tracer-standalone.html), [fringe-washout-standalone.html](fringe-washout-standalone.html), [planar-mpc-ray-tracer-standalone.html](planar-mpc-ray-tracer-standalone.html), [herriott-ray-tracer-standalone.html](herriott-ray-tracer-standalone.html), [onchip-herriott-standalone.html](onchip-herriott-standalone.html), [gas-cell-ray-tracer-standalone.html](gas-cell-ray-tracer-standalone.html), [multipass-chaos-lab-standalone.html](multipass-chaos-lab-standalone.html): complete HTML documents that open offline with no server, generated by `python tools/build_standalone.py`. |
| [cmpc-sim/](cmpc-sim/) | Chip-scale chaotic multipass cell simulator (Python): membrane evanescent coupling Γ, etched-trench DBR mirrors at oblique incidence, stadium and segmented-cell ray statistics, trace-gas Voigt spectra, speckle/etalon noise model; ten design figures. Built on the `membrane_mode`, `bragg_grating`, `billiard_cell`, `trace_gas` and `path_coherence` engines in `math-engines/`. |

| [general-mpc/](general-mpc/) | General multipass-cell simulator (Python; the `gmpc` modules wrap the `planar_cell` and `herriott_cell` engines): exact 3D tracing of Herriott, astigmatic (Lissajous) and slightly deformed Herriott cells (biconic/conic/polynomial mirror surfaces, tilt, decentre, radius and spacing errors, holes, apertures); planar cells of any wall (circle, polygon, smooth or faceted stadium, curved facets, integrated Herriott) with per-element perturbations; ports, path statistics, reflectance weighting, re-entrance and spot metrics, Lyapunov exponents. The engine tests cross-check `billiard_cell` and paraxial Herriott theory. |

## Shared engines

Plain, dependency-free JS modules (not standalone applets) meant to be
reused across multiple applets instead of re-derived each time.

| Folder | Contents |
|---|---|
| [beam-propagation/](beam-propagation/) | Polygon-billiard ray tracing and periodic-orbit solving for circular segmented multipass cells, plus an ABCD-matrix Gaussian-beam propagation engine (oblique-incidence astigmatism, periodic-system eigenmodes). Originally factored out of three `fys501-laser-physics` applets in [`mikkojhuttunen/physics-applets`](https://github.com/mikkojhuttunen/physics-applets). See its own README for the physics and API. |
| [waveguide-core/](waveguide-core/) | Photonic waveguide mode solver: Sellmeier materials, 1D slab solver, effective index method, 2D semi-vectorial finite-difference solver (usable inside a Web Worker). Used by the Er waveguide amplifier. |
| [nlo-dynamics/](nlo-dynamics/) | Nonlinear dynamics engine (`nlo-engine.js`): split-step Fourier solver for the nonlinear Schrödinger equation and its generalisation (Kerr, self-steepening, Raman, user-defined local nonlinearities, dispersion to any order, loss, gain filter), RK4/RK45 ODE integrators, pulse and spectrum analysis, and the Haus mode-locking round-trip model from the FYS.501 mode-locking explorer. 36-check validation suite, runnable examples. |
