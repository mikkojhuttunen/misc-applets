Dataset

E1 · Sensitivity per order vs launch angle

rms ∂I/∂am over all detector pixels (per unit coefficient, ÷ mean I₀) for a dot at the cell centre, asymmetric ports, one input; solid: fan ±5.7°, dashed: fan ±11.5°. A ray with incidence angle χ never comes closer to the centre than Rc sin χ, so only near-zero launches reach a centred dot. At zero launch the chords pass through the centre and see r(α) + r(α + π), so odd orders vanish; with a small offset (3°) they have sensitivity, but successive chords cancel it again and odd orders stay hard to read out (E13–E15).

E9 · Wave check: one pass through a dot, beamlet sums vs BPM off-centre archive

A beam crossing the cell (2 mm) through a 50 µm dot (edge 3 µm), observed beyond the far wall. ΔE is the scattered field E(dot) − E(no dot), the part that carries the dot's shape; correlation and relative error (after the best complex scale) of ΔE against split-step BPM. The FGA also matches exact Gaussian beams through clipping apertures and oblique mirror reflections (tests/test_fga.py).
