Dataset

Random deformed dots r(θ) = R[1 + Σ am cos mθ + bm sin mθ], m = 2…6, traced through the cell and ports set in the Explorer. Features: relative speckle intensity I/I₀ − 1 on every detector pixel, for every input port. Readout: ridge regression (the NGRC readout) on a 75/25 split, penalty chosen by validation.
