Physical numbers

Membrane mirror: Si index (Salzberg–Villa), neff of the air | Si | air slab mode and the oblique-incidence transfer matrix of cavity | [air gap, Si tooth] × N | air, ported from math-engines (as in mpc-schematics/dbr_periods.py and mpc-designs.html). 1D effective-index model: out-of-plane radiation in the gaps and roughness are not included.

Integrated Herriott cell: as mpc-schematics/herriott_planar.py; d = R(1 − cos 2πM/N), launch slope for xn = A cos(nθ + φ), exact 2D trace, trench-DBR reflectance per hit. Drawn with real cell dimensions in mm and an exaggerated DBR band and membrane thickness.

CRIGF: the geometry of crigf_schematic.m / dbr-structures/crigf_3d.py. One centred tooth per cell, tooth = fill × period, section = N × period; phase lengths are gaps between section edges; a positive offset moves the GC toward the right DBR. Defaults from Hemsley et al., Opt. Express 31, 27274 (2023); the 300 nm phase sections are example values. Additive rectangular teeth on a flat stack, no etch profile or optical field.
