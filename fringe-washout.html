How each parameter enters
  • Angle dither depth xθ = (dφ/dθ) Aθ. The slope dφ/dθ = k₀ n dΔL/dθ grows with the pass difference and the cell size, so the same Aθ dithers a long stray path much harder than a short one.
  • Spectral dither depth xν = 2π ng ΔL Δν / c: the number of free spectral ranges swept, times 2π. It needs ΔL Δν large: a 10 cm path with ng = 4.8 needs about 100 MHz for xν = 1.
  • Static floor |c₀(xθ) c₀(xν)|: what survives infinitely long averaging. For sine dithers |J₀(x)| ≲ √(2/(πx)), for triangle dithers |sinc(x)| ≤ 1/x. The two dithers multiply, so combining them is far more effective than pushing one.
  • Dither-rate ratio: if m fθ = n fν for small m, n (2:1, 3:2, 13:10), intermodulation products of those orders sit at 0 Hz and join the static floor. With deep dithers (x of tens) that can raise the floor a hundredfold; detuning one rate by a few Hz (gcd 1) removes it. Shallow dithers carry no power at high orders, so then it does not matter.
  • Averaging time: all harmonics at frequency f are reduced by about 1/(π f T) (boxcar) or 1/(2π f T) (RC). Seconds of averaging remove essentially everything except the static floor and very slow drifts.
  • Synchronous averaging: with a boxcar of exactly k common periods (1/gcd(fθ, fν)) every harmonic cancels exactly (the thin-curve nulls). Frequency jitter fills these nulls in practice; the bold curve is the robust estimate.
  • Drifts turn the static floor into a slow beat at ωd = k₀ ΔL (dn/dT)(dT/dt) + 2π ng ΔL (dν/dt)/c. Averaging longer than 1/fd then removes it too, but the drift also moves your baseline: a fringe that drifts during a measurement is noise, not offset.
  • Laser linewidth multiplies everything by |g| = exp(−π ΔνL ng ΔL / c). It matters only when ng ΔL approaches the coherence length c/(π ΔνL), 95 m for 1 MHz.
  • Stray power: the fringe amplitude in fractional intensity is 2√ε V; ε = −40 dB gives 2 % before any washout.
