Herriott Cell Ray Tracer

Exact 3D ray tracing between two real mirrors: spherical Herriott and astigmatic (Lissajous) designs, with tilt, decentre, radius, spacing, conic and surface errors. See the spot patterns, whether the beam still leaves through the injection hole after N passes, the tolerance on each error, the effective path, and the fringe washout under injection-angle dither.

Re-entrance tolerance

Distance of the exit point from the injection point in hole radii (left, log) and the re-entry angle (right) when everything else stays as set. Grey: the beam does not leave through the hole after exactly N passes (earlier through the hole, past the aperture, or still inside). A tilt or decentre of a spherical mirror only tilts the cell axis and keeps re-entrance; radius and spacing errors change θ and break it.

Intensity and effective path

Intensity Rʲ (left, log) and the absorption-weighted path Leff(j) = Σ Rⁱ ℓᵢ (right). The exit through the hole is not a reflection.

Twin-ray separation

Hit-point distance of two rays injected 1 nrad apart, holes closed. A stable two-mirror cell stays bounded: the separation oscillates instead of growing.

Phase slope |ap| = k₀ n |dLp/dθ| against pass number

A stable resonator refocuses: the slope oscillates with the pattern period instead of growing as in a polygon or exponentially as in a chaotic cell. Horizontal lines: slopes that give V = 0.9, 0.5, 0 at the current A.
