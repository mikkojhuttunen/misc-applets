# fringe_averaging

Residual visibility of one interference fringe (a stray path with optical path difference ΔL against the main light) after angle dithering, laser-frequency dithering, slow temperature and laser-frequency drifts, finite laser linewidth, and averaging over a measurement time T (boxcar or RC). Exact for any T via the harmonic (Jacobi-Anger) expansion: sine dither → Bessel J_m(x) (Miller recurrence, checked against scipy), triangle dither → closed-form coefficients; components at equal frequencies are summed exactly (dither frequencies in integer Hz). V_rms(T) = |g| √(Σ|C_Ω|² H(Ω,T)²) is the rms over the start of the averaging window; the Ω = 0 component is the static floor that no averaging removes: |g c₀(x_θ) c₀(x_ν)| plus every intermodulation product with m f_θ + n f_ν = 0 (orders (q k, −p k) for f_θ/f_ν = p/q). Dither rates in a low-order ratio can raise the floor by orders of magnitude at deep dithers (1000 and 1300 Hz: products of order 13 and 10; detuning to 1297 Hz lowers the floor 170× in the test case); `coincidence_orders` and `floor_split` report this. Checked against brute-force time averages, including commensurate dither frequencies and drift.

Version 1. `fringe_residual` returns a Result; `bessel_j_all`, `dither_harmonics`, `truncated_power`, `fringe_components`, `filter_gain` (with a lobe-averaged `envelope` option for the boxcar), `residual_visibility`, `common_period`, `coincidence_orders`, `floor_split`, `static_floor`, `depths`, `regular_cell_estimate`, `time_trace` are helpers. The applet `fringe-washout.html` carries a JS port checked against `test_vectors/vectors.json`.

Triangle dithers have corners, so their harmonics fall only as 1/m²; the cut-off keeps the neglected power near 1e-9. Depth limits: about 6 million dither-harmonic pairs (e.g. two sine dithers of depth ~1000 each).
