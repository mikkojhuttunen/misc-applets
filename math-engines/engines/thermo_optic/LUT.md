## Sources

- `si`: dn/dT: Komma et al., APL 101, 041905 (2012); TPA, n2: Bristow et al., APL 90, 191104 (2007); FCA: Soref & Bennett (1987); k: bulk 148, a 220 nm SOI film about 60-100 (phonon boundary scattering)
- `sio2`: dn/dT: Leviton & Frey, Proc. SPIE 6273 (2006), Malitson (1965); k: fused silica 1.38, PECVD/thermal oxide films 1.0-1.4
- `si3n4`: dn/dT: Arbabi & Goddard, Opt. Lett. 38, 3878 (2013); k: thin-film values 2.5-30 depending on film and thickness, bulk ceramic ~30
- `sinx`: Typical PECVD film values; n 1.8-2.1 and dn/dT both rise with Si content (Si-rich nitride: TPA and higher n2). Measure the film
- `ln_e`: dn/dT: Moretti et al., J. Appl. Phys. 98, 036101 (2005), 1523 nm, congruent; k, ρ, cp: crystal data sheets; alpha_L along c (15.4e-6 along a). Pyroelectric and photorefractive effects are not included
- `ln_o`: dn_o/dT is small and its sign differs between reports (Moretti 2005, Schlarb & Betzler 1993); alpha_L along a
- `lt_e`: dn/dT: estimate from temperature-dependent Sellmeier fits (Abedin & Ito 1996, Bruner et al. 2003 for stoichiometric LT); verify for the TFLT film. alpha_L along c (16.1e-6 along a)
- `lt_o`: As lt_e; ordinary-axis dn/dT is poorly known
- `gaas`: dn/dT: Della Corte et al., J. Appl. Phys. 88, 7115 (2000), 1.5 µm; TPA 6-26 cm/GW reported; k, ρ, cp: Adachi, GaAs and related materials (1993)
- `algaas`: Linear GaAs-AlAs interpolation; k: S. Adachi, J. Appl. Phys. 54, 1844 (1983). x >= 0.18 keeps 2hν(1550 nm) below the gap (no TPA, the usual choice for AlGaAs-on-insulator); Urbach-tail TPA close to the threshold is not modelled
- `inp`: dn/dT: Della Corte et al., J. Appl. Phys. 88, 7115 (2000); TPA at 1550 nm is non-zero (2hν > Eg) but not tabulated here
- `aln`: dn/dT: AlN ring-resonator measurements (Xiong et al. 2012, Pernice et al. 2012), about 2.3e-5; k: bulk single crystal 285, sputtered polycrystalline films 5-100+
- `al2o3_film`: n: reactive-sputtered Al2O3 1.60-1.67 at 1550 nm; k: ALD and sputtered films 1.0-3.3; dn/dT taken close to sapphire
- `sapphire`: dn/dT: Tropf & Thomas, Handbook of Optical Constants III (1998); k at 300 K 30-42
- `tio2_film`: Athermal Si/SiN waveguides with TiO2 cladding (Guha et al. 2013, Djordjevic et al. 2013); value depends on deposition and anneal
- `su8`: Typical polymer values; polymers have negative dn/dT of order -1e-4 1/K
- `air`: dn/dT = -(n - 1)/T at constant pressure; k of air at 300 K. Convection is not included
