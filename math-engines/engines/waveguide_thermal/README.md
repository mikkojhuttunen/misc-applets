# waveguide_thermal

Steady-state 2D heat conduction in a waveguide cross-section by finite volumes on a graded rectilinear grid (harmonic-mean face conductances, sparse direct solve with scipy). Isothermal substrate bottom (heat sink), convective top (h_top), adiabatic sides; only the half x ≥ 0 is solved.

- `ridge_heating(heat_per_length, core_material, core_width, core_height, slab_material, slab_thickness, box_material, box_thickness, substrate_material, substrate_thickness, clad_material, clad_thickness, domain_half_width, h_top, k_*, resolution, return_field)`: stack substrate / BOX / optional film / ridge or strip, cladding around it. Heat q′ (W/m) uniformly in the ridge. Returns the mean and peak ΔT, R′ = ΔT_core/q′, ΔT at the BOX and substrate tops, the energy time constant τ_E = ∫ρc_pΔT dA / q′, a heat-balance check and, with `return_field=True`, the grid and ΔT map. `k_core`, `k_slab`, `k_box`, `k_substrate`, `k_clad` override the LUT conductivities (thin-film or worst-case values).
- `thermal_shift(heat_per_length, dneff_dT, wavelength, n_group, length, **geometry)`: the same plus Δn_eff, phase over a length and a resonance shift.
- `ridge_dynamics(**geometry)`: response of the ridge temperature to a heat step (backward Euler, time step doubling) with 10/50/90 % times, and the exact frequency response of the discretised model with its −3 dB frequency. A 500 × 220 nm SOI wire on 2 µm BOX gives t₉₀ ≈ 10 µs and f₃dB ≈ 48 kHz.
- `ridge_mode(wavelength, **geometry)`: fundamental scalar mode on a finite-volume grid whose cell edges follow every interface (window around the ridge, substrate excluded).
- `mode_weighted_heating(heat_per_length, wavelength, heat_in_mode, **geometry)`: per-region Γ_r = ∂n_eff/∂n_r, dn_eff/dT of the channel waveguide, the mode-weighted ΔT and Δn_eff under the computed heating, n_g, and the resonance drift. With `heat_in_mode=True` the heat is deposited ∝ |E|² in the ridge.
- `self_consistent_heating(power, wavelength, loss_abs_db_per_cm, T_scale, beta_tpa, carrier_lifetime, sigma_fca, k_exponent_*, **geometry)`: iterates ΔT → n(T) → mode → absorbed heat (|E|² and |E|⁴ in the ridge) → k(T) → ΔT. It returns the converged temperatures, the exact Δn_eff of the re-solved mode against its first-order estimate, the change in A_eff and centroid (thermal lens), and a runaway flag. It matches the lumped `thermo_optic.thermal_runaway` to 1 % below threshold and diverges above it (tested).
- `axial_transfer(kappa, **geometry)` and `temperature_along_z(z, q, ends, **geometry)`: heat flow along the waveguide. R(κ) comes from the cross-section problem with a k κ² sink term (Fourier transform along z, exact for a z-invariant structure), and ΔT(z) is the FFT convolution of R(κ) with q′(z), for adiabatic chip ends or an unheated continuation. Checked against the buried-line image solution K₀(κ r) − K₀(2dκ). SOI: the axial length is ~1 µm, so the local 2D R′ holds for anything longer than a few µm.
- `heater_tuning(heater_width, heater_gap, heater_thickness, pitch, **geometry)`: P_π of a metal heater in the cladding, independent of length in 2D (SOI 2 µm heater 1 µm above the wire: 19 mW; thick SiN: ~120 mW), and thermal crosstalk to a waveguide at the given pitch.
- `mode_thermo_optic(...)` and `athermal_design(parameter, lower, upper, **geometry)`: channel-waveguide dn_eff/dT and dλ/dT (including substrate expansion); bisection for the width, height, film or BOX thickness that nulls dλ/dT under a negative-dn/dT cladding (e.g. a 1.2 µm wide SiN strip under SU-8: about 410 nm thick).
- **Modes**: `polarization="scalar" | "TE" | "TM"` in every mode-based function. TE and TM are semi-vectorial: n²E continuous across vertical or horizontal interfaces (Stern 1988), non-symmetric, solved with `eigs`. Their Γ_r, dn_eff/dT and Δn_eff come from re-solving the mode with perturbed indices, because the |E|² formula misses the TM field jump (it was 2× off for a Si slab). Tested against slab TE/TM n_eff (2e-4) and the exact slab dn_eff/dT (5 %). SOI 500 × 220 nm: n_eff TE/TM 2.50/1.86, dλ/dT 74/55 pm/K.
- **Heat sink and package**: `h_bottom` (W/m²K) puts an interface conductance (die attach, paste, TEC plate) between the substrate and the sink, in every solver.
- **Rectangles**: `rects=[(x0, x1, y0, y1, material), ...]` (y from the BOX top) paint air trenches, undercuts or metal (`METALS`: au, al, cu, w, pt, ti, tin, nicr) into the thermal geometry. `heater_tuning` uses them for `heater_material`, `trench_gap`/`trench_width`/`trench_depth` and `undercut_half_width`/`undercut_depth`. SOI 2 µm heater: P_π 18.6 mW plain, 14.6 mW with deep trenches 3 µm away, 0.63 mW with an undercut.
- `pulsed_heating(rep_rate, duty, **geometry)`: periodic steady state for a rectangular pulse train from the exact complex frequency response (Fourier series with Lanczos σ factors): peak, minimum and swing relative to CW. Agrees with superposed step responses to 0.2 %.
- `qpm_waveguide(pump_wavelength, signal_wavelength, temperature, length, rib geometry)`: TFLN rib n_eff at pump, signal and idler with the Gayer temperature Sellmeier; poling period, dΔk/dT and acceptance against bulk. 1.2 µm × 0.3 µm rib on 0.3 µm film, SHG 1550 nm: Λ = 3.76 µm, 1 cm acceptance 5.3 K (bulk 10.5 K).
- `solve_heat`, `conduction_matrix`, `build_ridge` (extra grid lines, rectangles) and `graded_axis` are the building blocks for other geometries.
- `tools/fem_crosscheck.py`: independent P2 finite-element solve (scikit-fem) of the same cross-sections. R′ agrees within 0.7 % for SOI, SiN and TFLN; the test is skipped when scikit-fem is not installed.

Checks: the mean step-response time against the exact moment of the discretised model (3 %), the scalar mode of a 12 µm wide strip against the slab TE solution, the Γ sum rule Σ Γ_r n_r = n_eff − λ ∂n_eff/∂λ, and the SOI drift (76 pm/K). Also a layered 1D stack against the exact solution (1e-4), a buried square in a homogeneous medium against the image (Carslaw & Jaeger) solution (3 %), grid convergence (2 %), heat balance, linearity, and the closed-form `thermo_optic.strip_thermal_resistance` within ±30 % for SOI, SiN and TFLN stacks.

Conductivities come from the `thermo_optic` LUT and are temperature independent. The model is 2D (uniform heating along z); for a pump that is absorbed along an amplifier use the local q′(z) (largest at the input) or the 3D extension in the TODO list.
