Planar cells are traced with flat and circular-arc wall elements (the general-mpc Cell2D picture; facets extended by 15 % past their corners). Random tilts use this page's own generator, so a seed here does not reproduce a numpy seed. Herriott cells are drawn from paraxial theory: xn = A cos nθx, yn = B sin nθy, cos θ = 1 − d/R, d = R(1 − cos 2πM/N).

Mirror: Si index from the Salzberg–Villa fit, neff of the air | Si | air slab mode, and the exact oblique-incidence transfer matrix of cavity | [air gap, Si tooth] × N | air, all ported from math-engines (materials, slab_waveguide, bragg_grating.TrenchDBR). It is a 1D effective-index model: radiation out of the membrane in the air gaps, sidewall roughness and TE↔TM conversion are not included, and above the first-gap TIR angle the model mirror is lossless.
