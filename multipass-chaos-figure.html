Multipass Chaos Figure

Composite paper figure from the Multipass Chaos Lab simulations: ray traces of the regular and chaotic circular (CMPC) and Herriott cells, their Poincaré sections, and the static residual fringe for each dither. The lab's engine runs here in the background, and the defaults are the lab's defaults. All figure text is Arial (Liberation Sans in the PDF); set the size below the figure.

The chaotic cells are the regular designs plus the index inclusions below. ε only scales the bars, so it redraws without a new run.

Export

The PDF is vector (fonts embedded) and the SVG carries its physical size in cm. jsPDF loads from cdnjs on the first PDF export. In the claude.ai viewer, confirm each file in the save dialog.

Settings used for the figure

What “Download settings” writes: the rail as set for the last run, the complete engine options of all four cells (SI units, angles in rad; the regular cells are the chaotic ones with the inclusions removed), the figure and display settings, and the values behind the bars. Rail changes made after the last run are flagged, not mixed in.
