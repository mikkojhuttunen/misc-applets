Multipass Chaos Lab

Two integrated multipass cells side by side: (A) a circular multipass cell with flat or curved facets and (B) a Herriott-type cell between two concave mirrors. Perturb facet and mirror curvatures and tilts, put an index disk in the middle, and see when the ray propagation turns chaotic, how the Poincaré section breaks up, and how far a launch-angle dither, a wavelength dither or both wash out the interference fringe and the exit speckle over the averaging time.

N_eff = (tr G)²/‖G‖²: the number of independent speckle patterns the dither averages over (exit field on phase-space channels of one mode each, G its coherence matrix over the dither cycle). Speckle contrast at the detector falls as 1/√N_eff; it cannot exceed the number of exit modes in use. Ring: current amplitudes.

Model and definitions
  • Cells (2D, in the slab plane, effective index n_eff): A, a regular polygon of flat facets (or 24 circular arcs for a smooth circle) with a facet curvature, random facet tilts and curvatures (rms, seeded) and one adjustable facet; input port mid facet 0, output port further along the wall. B, two concave profile mirrors at spacing L with the re-entrant design radius R = L/(1 − cos 2πM/N), one window on M1 for input and output, radius errors, tilts about each vertex, a quartic deformation and a sinusoidal ripple; optional reflecting side walls. Rays meet the mirrors exactly (safeguarded Newton).
  • Index perturbations: disks of index n_eff + Δn (the central one and random inclusions). Rays refract at their edges (Snell, total internal reflection; no Fresnel loss) and gain optical path n ℓ inside.
  • Input beam: B, the eigenmode of the ideal Herriott cell at the window; A, a Gaussian beam with its waist at the port. Port-to-port statistics use an n × n grid of rays from its Wigner function, each reflection multiplying by R.
  • Chaos: twin rays 10⁻⁹ rad apart (Lyapunov fit); finite-time Lyapunov exponent and MEGNO ⟨Y⟩ from a twin renormalised at every hit through walls and index disks (⟨Y⟩ → 0–2 regular, > 4 chaotic after 300 hits). Flat facets do not stretch the twin: their mixing comes from splitting at corners.
  • Fringe: the exit pass of the centre ray interferes with a stray copy whose path is shorter by a share of the exit path (stray setting); the copy follows the wavelength but not the angle. With the angle dither θ(t) at f_θ and the wavelength dither at f_λ (whole Hz) the term e^{i k(t) D(θ(t))} is periodic in both; an FFT over Na traced angle samples and the harmonics of the wavelength waveform at depth x = k₀ D Δλ/λ₀ give its components at m f_θ + n f_λ (equal frequencies summed; above 5 kHz pooled in 1/200-decade bins). The 0 Hz component is the static floor; a boxcar of length T weights the others by sinc(Ω T/2) (bold: lobe average 1/√(1 + 2z²)); a fringe drift ω_d shifts every frequency. Residual = 2√ε V/√M² with the speckle-averaging option.
  • Sampling floor: where the exit phase jumps between neighbouring angle samples (chaos, ray splitting), the floor cannot be resolved below about √π/(2√Na) (Na = 1024, 2048, 8192 by budget); the plateau there is an upper bound. The wavelength part is exact (tabulated harmonics, interpolated in x).
  • Speckle diversity: a fixed grid of frozen Gaussians (width = input radius) over offset and angle is traced once to the output port; a launch dither δ reweights them by ⟨g|E_in(δ)⟩, a wavelength step changes their phases k·OPL. Their fields add on exit channels of one mode (Δy Δ(n sin χ) = λ₀). Over the dither cycle (24 angle × 16 wavelength samples) G = Σ_t E_t E_t^†, N_eff = (tr G)²/‖G‖²_F, and the speckle contrast after averaging is 1/√N_eff of the frozen one. Approximations: no Herman–Kluk prefactor, no Gouy phase, beamlets as wide as the beam.
  • Absorption: A per ppm = 100 α Γ ⟨L⟩ with α of the strongest listed line; shot-noise NEC √(2qB/(P T))/A at 1 Hz, fringe NEC = 2√ε V_det/A.
  • PDF export: the PDF button on each figure redraws it as vector paths into a page of the same size (light theme) with the text in an embedded subset of DejaVu Sans Mono (Latin, Greek, super- and subscripts); jsPDF 2.5.1 is loaded from cdnjs on the first export, so exporting needs a network connection.
  • Not included: diffraction beyond the frozen-Gaussian and Gaussian-beam pictures, scattering, mirror coatings against angle, laser linewidth, intermodulation with other noise.
