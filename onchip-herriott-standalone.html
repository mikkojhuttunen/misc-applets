On-chip Herriott Cell

Two concave mirrors etched into a membrane, light guided in the slab between them. The cell's Gaussian mode rides on the traced ray; each bounce keeps the mirror's reflectance (constant, or an etched-trench DBR at the angle it meets) and the share of the beam that stays on the mirror and off the window. Read off the effective path, the mode volume the light samples, the angles at the mirrors and the throughput, and see how they change with the mirror reflectance.

Model and definitions
  • Design beam: the symmetric cell's Gaussian mode in the slab, λm = λ/neff, zR = √(d(2R − d))/2, w0² = λmzR/π, w(x) = w0√(1 + x²/zR²) along the cell axis. A mode-matched input keeps this width on every pass.
  • Effective path Leff = Σ Ij ℓj, with Ij the power on pass j (window coupling, reflectance and clipping of the hits before it). A weak absorber α removes α Leff of the input power.
  • Mode volume Veff = heff Σ Ij ∫ w(x)√(π/2) ds: the effective cross-section w√(π/2) × heff times the effective path. Aeff = Veff/Leff. Coverage: share of the area between the mirrors within one beam radius of the pattern.
  • Angles: ⟨|χ|⟩ and the RMS value are weighted by the power arriving at each reflection.
  • DBR: membrane | [air gap, tooth] × N | air, quarter-wave at the design angle, exact transfer matrix for the slab's lateral polarisation (TE → p, TM → s). Beam average: R(|χ| + α) weighted by the angular spectrum exp(−2α²/θ0²), θ0 = λm/(π w0).
  • Clipping: the 1D Gaussian share of each spot on the mirror (|y| below the half aperture) and, on M1 before the last pass, off the window. Throughput includes the window coupling in and out.
  • Waveguide loss α (dB/cm × 0.2303 per cm): the power on a pass decays as e−αs; Leff and Veff integrate that weight along every chord.
  • Layer stack: substrate | film | Er:Al₂O₃ | cladding, fundamental TE or TM slab mode by transfer matrices (same Sellmeier/Cauchy data as the Er amplifier applet); TFLN/TFLT use ne for TE and no for TM.
  • Beam propagation: the complex beam parameter q along the exact traced chords, with the in-plane (tangential) mirror power 2/(R cos χ) at each hit. The matched input is the eigenmode of the first round trip; its stability (A + D)/2 and Gouy phase are shown. Coupling: overlap of the input with the eigenmode at the window.
  • Erbium: the effective two-level model of the Er amplifier applet (980 nm absorbs only, 1480 nm also emits; upconversion, quenched ions; McCumber emission). The Er-layer intensity is P ΓEr/(w√(π/2) tEr), uniform over the thin layer and the beam's effective width. Crossing passes do not share inversion; no ASE.
  • Not included: diffraction at the mirror ends beyond clipping, mode conversion at the trenches, transverse overlap of crossing passes in the gain medium, ASE, thermal effects.
